## Clarifications carried into the tests

- A partial unique index protects unresolved operations. It does not prevent a late duplicate request after settlement. The request key remains unique for the lifetime of the record and a changed payload under that key is refused.
- Read-only funds and identity checks happen after durable admission and before the POST. A proven pre-submission rejection may close as `rejected`, with a safe reason code. An exception during or after the POST must never take that path.
- A persisted `submitting` record is never automatically replayed, even if the process died before actually sending. Failure to save a returned hash keeps that record and its protection.
- Dispatch has a durable timestamp and an atomic claim after preflight. A pre-dispatch terminal resolution prevents the claim. A resolver must use the same row's atomic state transitions. After a claim, absence of a transaction is never proof that the sender cannot still broadcast.
- History must survive a deployment rename or deletion. Recovery uses frozen identity rather than the deployment's current settings.
